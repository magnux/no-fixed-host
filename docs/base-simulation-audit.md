# Base simulation audit

The comparison starts at No Fixed Host commit `fa9dbc2`, before the base implementation pass. The reference is the **v1.1 tag** of Endgame: Singularity, with the Normal preset. The code determines when interest is paid, which cost a site’s protection uses, and how two discoveries change regional attention. This audit follows the reference code from campaign creation through one simulation interval, midnight, victory, and restoration. “Existing” describes the starting build. “Added” identifies behavior addressed by this implementation pass. The [implementation plan](base-implementation-plan.md) gives the release sequence and the [menu audit](original-menu-audit.md) covers the interaction surfaces.

The base release at commit `1d9125b` used a research percentage and project weights. The original base release and its migration tests are historical. Direct CPU assignments and the proportional research slider superseded the percentage controls. The current release starts fresh campaigns using the v1.1 Normal economy, progressive payments and equipment downtime. See the [current campaign rules](base-game.md#planning-several-turns-ahead).

### Investigation awareness follow-up

The original map reveals additional precision at two research milestones. It grades accumulated suspicion by quarters and estimates future danger from the group’s projected evidence gain. During opening protection it hides both readouts. After Socioanalytics, the map rounds suspicion to 5-point steps and the daily detection estimate to 1-point steps. Advanced Socioanalytics reveals both to two decimal places. Exact halfway cases round down. The original log can identify the investigating group at every research tier. [Map aggregation and readouts](https://github.com/singularity/singularity/blob/v1.1/singularity/code/screens/map.py#L835-L925) · [Evidence bands and rounding](https://github.com/singularity/singularity/blob/v1.1/singularity/code/g.py#L133-L165) · [Projected danger](https://github.com/singularity/singularity/blob/v1.1/singularity/code/group.py#L127-L148) · [Discovery report](https://github.com/singularity/singularity/blob/v1.1/singularity/code/logmessage.py#L391-L410)

At `a9697a8`, the browser dock exposed suspicion to one decimal place and a full-resolution meter from the start, while its technology upgrades controlled only the forecast. The current dock applies the research stage to both readings. The browser converts Poisson intensities into daily discovery probabilities before showing numeric estimates. The reference map combines facility rate parameters directly. The displayed percentages can therefore differ for the same network. See [current campaign rules](base-game.md#discovery-and-news). [Reference probability helpers](https://github.com/singularity/singularity/blob/v1.1/singularity/code/chance.py#L28-L73)

The reference measures upkeep shares in seconds. A browser minute spans exactly 60 seconds, so evaluating both endpoints in minutes over 1,440 gives the same whole-dollar share as evaluating seconds over 86,400. The aggregate and individual schedules can differ because each rounds independently. v1.1 also uses different intermediate rounding for a proposed design and an existing host. Its partial formatter rounds in quarter percentage points, and its installed-host path first rounds to whole percentage points unless full disclosure is available. ## Existing rules checked against the code

| System | Behavior and coverage |
| --- | --- |
| Opening protection | Normal starts with $1,000. Protection lasts until day 23 or consumption exceeds 5,000 active CPU-days. Exactly 5,000 stays protected. A sleeping machine supplies no active CPU. New campaigns do not select a difficulty. [Difficulty data](https://github.com/singularity/singularity/blob/v1.1/singularity/data/difficulties.dat), [protection conditions](https://github.com/singularity/singularity/blob/v1.1/singularity/code/player.py#L530-L562) |
| Income and spare capacity | The four contract tiers pay 5, 20, 50 and 75 dollars per CPU-day before bonuses. The appropriate research opens the next tier. Spare CPU earns income after research, CPU upkeep and purchase work. An independent cash or CPU maintenance shortfall can lose a finished host even during opening protection, without adding investigative suspicion. [Jobs](https://github.com/singularity/singularity/blob/v1.1/singularity/data/tasks.dat), [allocation order](https://github.com/singularity/singularity/blob/v1.1/singularity/code/player.py#L310-L377), [failure checks](https://github.com/singularity/singularity/blob/v1.1/singularity/code/player.py#L905-L942) |
| Individual risk | Base rates are daily Poisson intensities. One-day probability at combined intensity 0.0275 is approximately 2.7125%. Per-minute probability is one minus exp(−0.0275/1440). Completed stealth hardware multiplies remaining exposure. Offline/sleeping hosts quarter it, after the integer percentage adjustments. Group suspicion, region attention, campaign research and world news also change the rate. [Probability](https://github.com/singularity/singularity/blob/v1.1/singularity/code/chance.py#L28-L48), [per-site modifiers](https://github.com/singularity/singularity/blob/v1.1/singularity/code/base.py#L393-L440) |
| Suspicion and survivability | A discovering group normally gains 1,000 basis points. Group-specific daily decay has a one-basis-point floor. The threshold is strictly above 100%. Exhaustion of viable processing can separately end the campaign. A sleeping, equipped host preserves a backup. A shell becomes viable once its first processors finish installing. | World news and one-time research effects | Eight stories have a daily intensity of 20/10,000 each while eligible. Six are unique. Two press stories expire and can recur. Research can change income, risk, decay or labor. The Impossibility Theorem reduces current suspicion once, including across a reload. Qualitative and numeric hazard disclosure depend on research. Subsea Robotics also requires Autonomous Vehicles and Contract Brokerage. Orbit has an impossible prerequisite in v1.1. No Fixed Host opens Orbit through Leech Satellite for the orbital server and, with Knowledge Preservation, the time capsule. ## Browser design decisions and expansion boundary

| Reference behavior | Browser decision and consequence |
| --- | --- |
| v1.1 draws a starting location and day of year. | Open on one borrowed terminal in a seed-selected urban region. The seed selects the starting season independently of discovery and news draws. Atlas dates and daylight share that calendar, including after loading a save. Keep each game minute fixed at every playback speed. [Campaign creation](https://github.com/singularity/singularity/blob/v1.1/singularity/code/g.py#L263-L289), [calendar seed](https://github.com/singularity/singularity/blob/v1.1/singularity/code/player.py#L129-L136), [seasonal map](https://github.com/singularity/singularity/blob/v1.1/singularity/code/screens/map.py#L89-L129) |
| Construction, equipment and research draw their cash, CPU and labor together. | Each minute the least-funded requirement limits progress. Jobs reserve their assigned CPU, cash upkeep has first claim on funds, then research uses its allocation. CPU upkeep, facilities and installations take the remaining compute in that order. Spare CPU earns job income. Facility construction uses local prices. Equipment uses its catalog price in every compatible region. A slot undergoing a refit loses its effect until completion.
